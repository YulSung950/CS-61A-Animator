Extension 6 — Mirror duel. After any successful draw, the animator builds a
junction graph from the current paths so branched drawings (for example tree)
keep real forks instead of a single flattened polyline. Entering Mirror duel
(under-canvas toggle) shows the full course and places two riders on it: the
player starts near the beginning of the drawing and the opponent near the end.
Movement is hold-to-move only with no coasting or momentum after key release:
W or Up advances forward along the current path while held and stops when
released; S or Down moves backward while held and stops when released; A/D or
Left/Right steer onto a new branch at a split, and when no turn key is held the
rider prefers the furthest-right outgoing path by default. In vs-AI mode either
WASD or the arrow keys steer the player against a reactive AI. Hot-seat is
player-versus-player with separated bindings: WASD controls one rider and the
arrow keys control the other, so both schemes no longer drive the same rider.
Collision ends the round with a spark burst and a CRASH banner; Space rematches.
Exiting duel restores the normal timed reveal. Drawing select (including the
circle_spiral library seed), shape/speed sliders, error overlay, and clock
reset on code change keep working with duel on or off.

Extension 10 — Music sync. A Web Audio pipeline plays a short built-in kick/hat
loop (or a user-chosen audio file). An AnalyserNode measures energy each frame:
beats pulse set_speed briefly and brighten stroke colors on the canvas;
sensitivity is adjustable next to Play/Pause on the under-canvas strip. Optional
Bass→Shape maps low-band energy onto the shape slider so the drawing morphs with
the bass while music plays. Pausing restores the user-chosen base speed and
normal brightness without disturbing the duel or Q1–Q3 controls.
